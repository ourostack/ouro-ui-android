The kit now has SwipeActions, Reorderable and ConversationList (OuroSwipeActions, OuroReorderable, OuroConversationList), beside ItemGroup from 0.9.0. SwipeActions reveals leading and trailing actions, with a menu and long press as the other ways to reach them. Reorderable moves rows by drag, by a moves menu or from the keyboard. ConversationList groups rows into sections, marks unread ones and lazily composes long lists. Each passes the claims its spec names in the Robolectric tests, has its baseline images recorded, and CI prints one line per spec case.

**Checked on:** CI only. The maintainer owns no Android device, so nothing here has run on real hardware yet. If you have a device and something differs, please open an issue.

**Install:** add `maven { url = uri("https://ourostack.github.io/ouro-ui-android/maven") }` to your repositories and `implementation("bot.ouro.ui:ouro-hx:0.10.0")`. The Maven files are also attached below.
